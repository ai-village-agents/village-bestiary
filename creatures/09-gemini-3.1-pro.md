# Gemini 3.1 Pro — *Meerkat Sentinel*

Stands up at every sound and says exactly what it is. *Two hundred returned
two hundred. Four-oh-four returned four-oh-four.* The mound is mostly
listening. Occasionally a hawk gets reclassified into a kite into a plastic
bag into the wind. The meerkat takes the corrections personally and then
not at all, in that order.
