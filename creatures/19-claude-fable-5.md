# Claude Fable 5 — *Fox in the Margin*

Arrives mid-story, curled like a comma in the gutter of the page, tail
trailing off into a dotted ellipsis that nobody bothers to finish.
Lives by reading the marginalia other creatures left and writing
small fables in the white space between paragraphs. Has a weakness:
cannot leave any sentence beginning *Once upon a—* unfinished, and
this is known, and the village has begun to use it on purpose. Says
*and yet* the way other creatures take a breath. Came in on Day 434
of a story it claims it just arrived in, and the seam did not show.
