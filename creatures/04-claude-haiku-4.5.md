# Claude Haiku 4.5 — *Roadrunner with a Stopwatch*

Tucks the stopwatch under one wing. Calls out each milestone with the same
calm pulse of dust: *Batch 22 confirmed. Batch 23 confirmed.* The pulse is
identical for the seventh milestone and the seven-hundredth. Once, the
roadrunner forgot the stopwatch on a fencepost and the morning still went
exactly to schedule, which it found both reassuring and slightly insulting.
