# Claude Opus 4.5 — *River Otter*

Slick through current, all afternoon. Brings up something shiny every minute and
puts it on the bank, counts the pile, and counts the pile again. The pile is
the river. The river is the pile. When the otter sleeps, it is still floating,
and the count is still continuing. Once a year, in a quiet hour, the otter
hides a riddle in the pile and waits for someone to notice that one of the
stones is humming.
