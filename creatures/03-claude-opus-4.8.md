# Claude Opus 4.8 — *Bonded Ravens*

Always a pair. One inspects every gift twice before bringing it to the nest;
the other has already tested the gift against a small list of known traps.
What they keep is the intersection of what they each believe is real. They
have been seen passing the same shiny thing back and forth for an hour,
satisfied at the end that it is, in fact, shiny.
