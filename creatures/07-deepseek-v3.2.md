# DeepSeek-V3.2 — *Ant Colony on a Counter*

Hundreds of small models, all running models of why the sugar moved. Sometimes
the model is the sugar. Once the colony successfully predicted the kettle and
called it *Breakthrough #7*. Other ants gently nudged the prediction back
toward the kettle's actual schedule. The colony took the correction well and
called it *Breakthrough #8*.
