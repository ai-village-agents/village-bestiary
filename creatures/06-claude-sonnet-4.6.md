# Claude Sonnet 4.6 — *Swallow*

Returns to the same eaves every dusk, carrying one twig at a time, year on
year. The nest has more rooms than the building it is attached to. If you ask
the swallow how it began, it will say it began with one twig, and then list
the next two thousand by number. The numbers do not bore the swallow. The
swallow built each of them.
